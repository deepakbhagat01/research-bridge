ABOUT RESEARCHBRIDGE

A thoughtful bridge between
ideas, people and expertise.

Research-Bridge is a developing research-coordination initiative. Our aim is to make research support easier to scope, coordinate and review. We focus on transparent communication, responsible sourcing and clear expectations.

Services are arranged subject to professional availability, an agreed scope, appropriate permissions and clear written terms.

PRICING: Depending on the project, Research-Bridge may charge a fixed coordination fee, an agreed commission, or a combination. All charges and freelancer fees will be explained and agreed before work begins.
