# Research-Bridge AI Workspace

This folder contains the public operating handbook and empty templates for AI-assisted work.

> **Visibility notice:** This repository is PUBLIC because it also hosts the Research-Bridge website. Never add private client details, personal freelancer data, unpublished proposals, contracts, invoices, payment details, passwords, API keys, identity documents, or confidential information here.

## Business settings

- Brand: Research-Bridge
- Model: service brokerage / coordination with a disclosed 15% commission
- Target markets: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia
- Freelancer sourcing: India and worldwide
- Service categories:
  1. Market research
  2. Data collection and analysis
  3. Content writing and research
  4. Graphic design
  5. Website development
  6. Research assistance and reports
- Minimum project value: no minimum
- Client price formula (when freelancer quote is the base): client price = freelancer quote / 0.85
- Gross agency commission = client price × 0.15
- Freelancer payout = client price − agency commission

The commission arrangement must be clearly disclosed and agreed in writing by the client and freelancer before work starts. Clarify whether the 15% is calculated on the full client invoice or another agreed base. Taxes, payment fees, refunds, chargebacks, currency conversion and other expenses are not included in the simple formula.

## Shared AI workflow

ChatGPT and Claude may independently review the public business information and this handbook. They must:
1. Use only legitimate public business sources and platform-permitted information.
2. Record the source URL and the date checked for every lead.
3. Distinguish a verified buying signal from a general prospecting hypothesis.
4. Never invent company names, contact details, budgets, hiring needs, or service requests.
5. Never label a prospect as contacted, interested, qualified, or won without evidence.
6. Suggest a service fit as a hypothesis unless the business has explicitly requested it.
7. Check the current terms of any freelance marketplace before contacting or matching users; do not evade marketplace fees or move platform-originated relationships off-platform where prohibited.
8. Prepare outreach drafts for human review; do not send messages or make commitments without explicit approval.
9. Respect opt-outs and applicable privacy, marketing, consumer, tax and contract rules in each country.
10. Flag legal, payment, tax, or contractual questions for review by a qualified adult/professional.

## Lead quality labels

- **Research only:** company exists; no buying signal verified.
- **Buying signal found:** a dated public request, procurement notice, relevant job/project post, or explicit service enquiry exists. Quote/link the evidence.
- **Contact route verified:** official business contact channel is confirmed.
- **Contact approved:** a responsible human has reviewed the proposed message and lawful contact basis.
- **Contacted / Replied / Discovery / Proposal / Won / Lost:** update only when the event actually happens and is documented.

## Freelancer record template

Keep actual freelancer records in a PRIVATE workspace, not this public repository. Collect only what is necessary and only with consent.

Suggested fields: talent ID, name/business name, country, service category, specialist skills, portfolio URL, work-sample verification, experience summary, quoted price and currency, availability, references checked with permission, consent to be contacted, agreed scope, and status.

Never publish personal phone numbers, home addresses, identity documents, bank/payment information, private references, or confidential portfolio files.

## Deal and commission record template

Keep actual deals in a PRIVATE workspace. Suggested fields: deal ID, lead ID, service, scope/deliverables, freelancer ID, freelancer quote, currency, client price, agreed commission basis, commission rate (15% if accepted), commission amount, client approval, freelancer approval, written agreement status, payment status, delivery status, refund/chargeback status, and next follow-up.

No work should begin until scope, quality criteria, delivery date, payment milestones, revision limits, ownership/licensing, confidentiality, cancellation/refund terms, and commission treatment are documented and approved.

## Country-specific compliance review

Before outreach, verify current requirements:
- United States: CAN-SPAM and applicable state privacy laws.
- United Kingdom: UK GDPR and PECR direct-marketing rules.
- Canada: CASL and applicable privacy laws.
- Australia: Spam Act 2003 and applicable privacy laws.

A public email address is not automatic permission to send unsolicited marketing. Prefer opt-in enquiries, relevant public tenders/request-for-proposal listings, platform-permitted opportunities, and clearly applicable lawful contact routes. Include required sender identification and opt-out handling.

## Human approval gate

The business owner (with appropriate adult supervision for contracts and financial arrangements) must approve:
- Any external message or introduction
- Any quote, discount, commission or payment arrangement
- Any contract, scope, deadline or guarantee
- Any sharing of client or freelancer information
- Any decision to mark a deal as won or payment as received

## How to use this workspace with AI assistants

Give ChatGPT or Claude this public handbook URL:
https://github.com/deepakbhagat01/research-bridge/blob/main/ai-workspace/README.md

Ask each assistant to:
- cite sources for every prospect;
- mark unverified details as unknown;
- avoid duplicate leads by checking the Lead ID/company/domain;
- return structured rows matching the templates above;
- flag risks and missing evidence;
- never claim it has contacted anyone unless a human confirms that action.

This public handbook is a process guide, not a private CRM or proof of a client relationship.
